# The Tide Bell

## Synopsis

Teodor gives the girl the tide bell and tells her what its ringing means.

## Beats

1. The bell changes hands.
2. Someone is watching from the marsh road.

## Prose

Teodor presses the tide bell into the girl's hand. "Keep it," he says. "When it rings, the crossing is closed, and nobody goes out." She closes her fingers over it and does not let go.

From the road that runs along the Glasswater marsh, Brother Halvard watches them, muttering the Rite of Brine under his breath the way he does before every drowning.
