# The Gull

## Synopsis

A gull wakes Teodor before the tide, and he is short with it or kind to it.

## Beats

1. The gull wakes Teodor.
2. Teodor argues with the harbourmaster over the mooring fee.

## Prose

A gull lands on the sill and screams at Teodor until he sits up. He has been tired for days now, a little more each morning, and he tells the gull so.

Option 1: "Off with you." He shoos it out the window. -1 to the gull's variable

Option 2: "All right, I'm up." He tears it a corner of bread. +1 to the gull's variable

The gull stays or goes, and Teodor pulls on his coat.
