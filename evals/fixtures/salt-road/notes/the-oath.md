# The Oath

Teodor has never known who his own parents were; the Salt Road raised him, and he stopped asking long ago.

He finds her at the landing after dark, humming to the tide with the bell in her lap. He sits down beside her, and before he says anything else he asks, "Can you keep a secret?"

The girl is prompted yes/no, with either leading to the same line: "Good. Then I'll tell you where the bell came from." He tells her he took it the night the crossing closed, and that the Wardens would hang him for it if they knew.
