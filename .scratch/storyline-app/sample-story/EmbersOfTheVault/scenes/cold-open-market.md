# Cold Open: Lowmarket

## Synopsis

A grain lift in Lowmarket goes sideways when the Warden's tollmen sweep the stalls a bell early. Rook gets out with the ledger scrap that starts everything.

## Beats

1. The tollmen arrive a bell early — someone tipped them.
2. Rook trades the grain for a way out through Petch's tunnel.
3. The ledger scrap: a Vault requisition, signed by the Warden himself.

## Prose

The tollmen came at the wrong bell, which meant somebody had sold the right one.

Rook was under the fishmonger's awning with forty pounds of grain in a coffin-sized handcart when the whistles started, and by the second whistle had already decided the grain was somebody else's problem.
