# The Drowned Chapel

## Synopsis

A flooded chapel where the lamplighters leave offerings. Whoever waits out a full tide there learns a name they weren't supposed to.

## Beats

1. The offerings are all lamp-wicks, knotted in the counting pattern.
2. The name arrives by echo, not by voice.
