# Ashes or Embers

## Synopsis

What the Vault held, and what the city decides to do about it. The endings braid: the uprising, Mara, and the Warden's fall or flight.

## Beats

1. The Vault's secret is not gold.
2. The founding lie, read aloud, in the open air.
3. The Warden's eye, closed one way or another.
