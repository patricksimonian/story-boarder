# Smoke Over Lowmarket

## Synopsis

The cross-arc payoff. `a4_hearths_lit` was written in the Kindling lane and is read here, in a lane that never references it by name in prose — the variable is the only thread between the two arcs, and it's enough, because variables are story-global. The tanner relights his neighbor's hearth from his own; the mill children carry coals door to door. Kindness written in one arc becomes weather in another. This is the shape for "her saving the plants affects the second arc, not her arc."

## Beats

1. Two chimneys smoking where there were none
2. Fire passed hand to hand, nobody asking whose it was first

## Prose

Nobody in Lowmarket knows where the first two fires came from. By the third morning it doesn't matter.
