# Lowmarket Rises

## Synopsis

The pamphlets did their work. Lowmarket rises while the Warden is looking down at the Vault instead of up at the city.

## Beats

1. The counting-song changes words, and every lamplighter knows the new verse by dusk.
2. The tollhouses burn their own ledgers to have nothing to surrender.
