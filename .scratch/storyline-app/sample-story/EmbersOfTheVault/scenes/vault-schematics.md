# Three Bad Doors

## Synopsis

Three ways into the Vault, all bad. The crew argues the drowned service stair into being the plan.

## Beats

1. The front door: ceremonial, watched, absurd.
2. The coin chute: too narrow for anyone but Petch, who refuses.
3. The drowned service stair: forgotten, flooded, and therefore the plan.
