# The Orphan Mill

## Synopsis

Same pattern as Tanner's Row, second chance to spend. Every gift is one `+= 1` on the same counter — the engine doesn't care which hearths, only how many. If a *particular* gift ever mattered on its own, that's when it would earn its own boolean; until something later tests it, a count is all the story needs.

## Beats

1. The mill children keep watch in shifts against the cold
2. An ember passed through the fence, or not

## Prose

The mill's stove has been cold so long the children have forgotten it's a stove. Rook's hand rests a moment on the coat pocket.
