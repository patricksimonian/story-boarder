# The City Gate

## Synopsis

The last stretch before the road — and deliberately choice-less. A choice names its destination, and if that destination's gate is shut the choice just shows closed; only the lane's quiet continuation knows how to walk *past* a shut gate to the first open scene. So this scene carries no choices, and the lane itself decides what comes next: The Warm Welcome if the count was earned, The Road North if not.

## Beats

1. The gate guards wave the crowd of the displaced through
2. Rook joins the line, lighter or heavier than an hour ago

## Prose

The gate stands open for once — too many people leaving to bother stopping one more.
