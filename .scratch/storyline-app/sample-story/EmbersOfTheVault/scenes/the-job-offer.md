# The Job Offer

## Synopsis

In the back room of the Brass Lamp, Dax lays out the Vault job and introduces the safecracker: Mara. She agrees before hearing the cut — too fast.

## Beats

1. The Vault has never been opened while the city stood.
2. Mara agrees before hearing the cut — too fast.
3. Rook clocks the Warden's brand on the inside of her wrist.

## Prose

The back room of the Brass Lamp smelled of lamp oil and older lies. Dax spread the requisition flat with two fingers, the way a priest handles a relic he privately doubts.
