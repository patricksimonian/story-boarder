# Cold City

## Synopsis

The second arc opens, and notice what it doesn't do: it never mentions Rook, the embers, or the walk. This lane is about the city the crew left behind. It's ungated so the arc always has a foothold — a lane whose first scene is gated has nowhere to start from.

## Beats

1. Winter settles on a city that shares nothing
2. Doors bolted, hearths hoarded

## Prose

The frost comes early to Lowmarket, and this year nobody's giving anything away.
