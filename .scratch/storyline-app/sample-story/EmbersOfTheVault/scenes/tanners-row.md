# Tanner's Row

## Synopsis

Rook slips out of the burning quarter with a coat full of live embers and passes the tanner's family huddled at a dead hearth. This is the walk-scene pattern: two choices to the same next scene, one carrying `a4_hearths_lit += 1`, the other carrying nothing. The moment a scene has any choice, the quiet continue-along-the-lane stops — so "Keep to the shadows" isn't decoration, it's the road.

## Beats

1. The burning quarter at Rook's back, the cold city ahead
2. The tanner's children pressed against a hearth gone grey

## Prose

The embers ride warm against Rook's ribs, each one a coal from the Vault's own fire. Through a shutter gap: three children and a grate with nothing in it.
