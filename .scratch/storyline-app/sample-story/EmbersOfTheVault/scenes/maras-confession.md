# The Dry Cistern

## Synopsis

Camped in the dry cistern, Mara tells Rook what the Warden really holds over her — and what she plans to do about it at the door.

## Beats

1. Mara talks first, which has never once happened.
2. Her brother isn't a hostage. He's a wardsman — he chose the eye.
3. What she wants from the Vault isn't inside it. It's the opening itself.

## Prose

The cistern had been dry for a hundred years and still remembered water; every sound came back twice. Rook fed the little stove. Mara watched the door out of habit, then stopped watching it, which from her was a speech.
