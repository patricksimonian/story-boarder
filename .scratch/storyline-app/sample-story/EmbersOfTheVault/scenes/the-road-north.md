# The Road North

## Synopsis

The destination — the cutscene analog — and it is *ungated*, which is the whole point. This scene always happens; putting the condition here would have made the spine of the act conditional on a side kindness. Generous or not, Rook walks north. What differs is the scene before it, not this one.

## Beats

1. The city smaller with every step
2. What the coat still carries

## Prose

The road bends away from the smoke. Rook doesn't look back until looking back costs nothing.
